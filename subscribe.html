FAQ

Why is the public index frozen on Friday?

The Loan Clarity Borrower Sentiment Index updates continuously behind the scenes, but the public dashboard shows only the most recent Friday reading. This protects the proprietary signal flow while still giving everyone a free, weekly snapshot via Sentiment Friday. Pro subscribers see the live data as it moves.

What's actually in the Pro tier?

Pro subscribers get: (1) live index — updated every 5 minutes instead of every 7 days, (2) the full archive of past Sentiment Friday issues, (3) the weekly deep-dive section (Pro-only commentary on what's moving the signal), (4) 90 days of historical data for backtesting against your own portfolio data, and (5) an ad-free reading experience.

What happens after I pay?

You get instant access to the Pro dashboard, today's live index reading, and the full back-issue archive. The Friday newsletter you already subscribed to continues — now with bonus Pro-only sections.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. After 14 days, cancel anytime and you keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

What's the difference between Pro and Enterprise?

Pro is for individuals and small teams — analysts, advisors, researchers, journalists. Enterprise is for institutional integrations — lenders running the data in production, hedge funds wiring it into SLABS models, rating agencies blending it into credit factors. Enterprise includes API access, custom segments, white-label embeds, and methodology disclosure under NDA. Apply at /api.
