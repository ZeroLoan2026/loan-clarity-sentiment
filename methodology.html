Methodology

How the Loan Clarity Borrower Sentiment Index™ is calculated · Last revised May 2026

00Overview

The Loan Clarity Borrower Sentiment Index™ is a single 0–100 score that measures the real-time emotional and financial stress of US student loan borrowers. 0 represents broad financial optimism; 100 represents extreme distress.

It blends six independently-sourced signals — three real-time, three institutional — into one authoritative reading, refreshed every five minutes. The methodology is intentionally transparent: every weight, source, and computation step is published below.

Design Principle

No single data source should dominate the index. By design, the largest single weight is 20% and the smallest is 10%. This prevents any one platform's outage, API change, or measurement bias from skewing the headline reading.

01Weighted Index Formula

The headline score is a weighted sum of six signal scores, each on a 0–100 scale:

Index = 0.20 × Google_Trends_Panic + 0.20 × Reddit_Sentiment + 0.15 × CFPB_Complaints + 0.15 × Delinquency_Trends + 0.10 × Refinance_Demand + 0.20 × Borrower_Surveys # clamped to [5, 95] to avoid false-extreme readings from one-off spikes

02Google Search Panic Index

We poll Google Trends for the search query can't pay student loans at a four-week rolling average, restricted to the United States. Google returns a relative interest score of 0–100 where 100 is peak historical interest.

That raw index maps directly to our panic score — high search volume for distress queries indicates high borrower anxiety. We clip outliers above 95 to avoid post-injunction spike bias.

03Reddit Sentiment (AI-Classified)

Each refresh cycle:

STEP 01
Fetch
Pull top 50 hot posts from r/StudentLoans, r/personalfinance, r/povertyfinance via Reddit's public JSON endpoint.
STEP 02
Filter
Keep only posts whose titles contain one of 16 student-loan keywords (SAVE, IDR, PSLF, MOHELA, etc.).
STEP 03
Rank
Sort by engagement score = upvotes + 2 × comments. Keep top 25.
STEP 04
Classify
Send top 18 titles to the Loan Clarity AI sentiment engine — a large-language-model pipeline producing a 0–100 sentiment score plus five emotion probabilities.

04CFPB Complaint Volume

We query the CFPB's public complaints API for filings tagged Product = "Student loan" in the last 90 days. The raw count is normalized:

CFPB_Score = clip( complaints_90d / 500, 0..100 )

500 complaints/day at the 90-day mark = score of 100. Currently the system is reading a spike of +320% YoY following the SAVE plan injunction.

05Delinquency Trends

Pulled from the NY Fed's quarterly Household Debt & Credit Report. We use the share of student loan accounts 90+ days past due. The Q1 2026 reading of 5.7% maps to a delinquency score of 72.

This signal is updated quarterly; between releases it remains pinned at the most recent value.

06Refinance Demand

Counter-intuitively, high refinance search demand is a distress signal — it means borrowers are actively trying to exit federal repayment. We measure YoY growth in "refinance student loans" search interest via Google Trends.

07Borrower Surveys

Survey signal comes from the most recent Pew Research student loan borrower survey. We use the percentage of respondents reporting they feel "worried" about their student loan situation. Current reading: 80% → score of 80.

Future phases will add a proprietary panel survey of 5,000 borrowers for higher resolution.

08AI Emotion Classification

For each batch of Reddit posts, the Loan Clarity AI sentiment engine returns five emotion probabilities on a 0.0–1.0 scale: anxiety, confusion, frustration, optimism, hopelessness. These are displayed as horizontal bars on the dashboard.

The "Status" line color is set by whichever emotion has the highest probability — making the dashboard's mood instantly readable.

Engine Design

The sentiment engine runs on a large language model with structured-output prompting, pinned to a fixed model snapshot for score stability. Each classification costs roughly $0.002 and completes in under a second, enabling true real-time refresh without compromising on linguistic nuance.

Why It Matters

A borrower-sentiment index is most useful when it can be triangulated against the macro backdrop. Rising unemployment, rising Treasury yields, and falling consumer sentiment all amplify the headline reading — these indicators tell the analyst why the index is moving, not just where it is.

10Normalization & Scoring Model

All six signals are normalized to the same 0–100 stress scale before weighting:

  • Direct mapping — Google Trends (already 0–100) and survey % map 1:1.
  • Linear scaling — CFPB complaints / 500; delinquency % × ~13.
  • AI output — the sentiment engine returns a score directly on the 0–100 scale.
  • Clipping — final index clipped to [5, 95] to absorb one-off spikes.

12Known Limitations

  • Reddit selection bias — Reddit borrowers skew younger and more financially literate than the full borrower population.
  • Google Trends rate limiting — Occasional 429s force fallback to the most recent known value.
  • Quarterly signals lag — Delinquency and enrollment data is 6–10 weeks behind real time by structural cadence.
  • LLM sentiment drift — model updates can shift sentiment scores slightly; the engine is pinned to a fixed model snapshot to mitigate this.
