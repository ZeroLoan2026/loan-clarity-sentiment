FAQ

  

    Is the embed free?
    
Yes. Forever. We don't charge for embeds because every embed is a backlink and a citation — that's value for us, not a cost. No license fee, no rate limit, no signup.

  

  

    Does it update automatically?
    
Yes. The iframe refreshes every 5 minutes, so readers always see the current index reading whether they land on your article today or six months from now.

  

  

    Will it slow down my page?
    
No. The iframe loads in under 200ms, has zero external dependencies (no jQuery, no React, no fonts loaded from CDNs), and doesn't block your page render. It's just one HTTP request to a static-cached HTML document.

  

  

    Is there a non-iframe / JavaScript option?
    
For most editorial use (NYT, WSJ, Bloomberg, Forbes), iframe is preferred because their CMS blocks third-party scripts. If you need a JS-based or React-component version for tighter integration, email zeroloan000@gmail.com — we can ship it within 48 hours.

  

  

    Can I cite this in research or journalism?
    
Yes — please do. Suggested citation: "Loan Clarity Borrower Sentiment Index, [DATE], studentloansindex.com". Email us if you'd like a formal data partnership or attribution arrangement.

  

  

    What about a custom-branded version?
    
White-label embeds (your logo, your colors, removing the Loan Clarity branding) are available on the Enterprise tier. See pricing.
