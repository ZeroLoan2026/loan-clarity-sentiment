WHAT CHANGES

The rules borrowers aren't ready for.

Plain English on what shifts at 12:01 AM ET on July 1, 2026 — and why each change will move the index.

⚖️

SAVE plan resolution

The court's narrow ruling earlier this year leaves the SAVE plan structurally weakened. On July 1, transition rules begin for 8 million enrolled borrowers — most of whom haven't read the notices.

Likely index move: +3 to +6 points within 14 days. Reddit signal will spike first.
📈

Interest accrual changes

Borrowers in transitional repayment plans will see interest behave differently. For some, monthly payments rise sharply. Servicer notice quality has been inconsistent.

Likely index move: CFPB complaint trajectory accelerates. Google search panic +20-30%.
📝

IDR recertification deadlines

Income-Driven Repayment recertification windows compress. Borrowers who miss windows get bumped to standard repayment — sometimes 3-5× their current payment.

Likely index move: Survey signal jumps. Delinquency print 60-90 days later.
🏛️

Servicer transitions

Several portfolios are being reassigned between servicers. Historically, every major transition produces a CFPB complaint spike within 30 days as account access breaks.

Likely index move: Sharp CFPB signal spike. Watch MOHELA, Nelnet, Aidvantage handoffs.
🎓

PSLF certification changes

Public Service Loan Forgiveness recertification rules tighten. ~2M PSLF borrowers — teachers, nurses, government workers — will need to reverify or risk losing accumulated credits.

Likely index move: Distinct sentiment cohort emerges. Reddit theme shifts.
💸

Default protocols update

Default treatment timelines accelerate. Wage garnishment notices now arrive faster after missed payments. Rehabilitation paths narrow.

Likely index move: Reddit "wage garnishment" mentions climb. Refinance search demand drops (can't refi federal loans).
