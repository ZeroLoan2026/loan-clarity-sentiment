## 🚨 The July 1 thing

If you're reading this on Friday, you have **34 days** before the next regulatory pile-on. Quick version of what changes:

- **SAVE plan transition rules kick in** — 8M borrowers in this plan. Most haven't gotten clear guidance.
- **Interest accrual gets weirder for some borrowers** — payments go up. Servicer notices have been inconsistent.
- **IDR recertification windows compress** — miss it, get bumped to standard repayment (often 3-5× current payment).
- **Servicer transitions** — historically, every handoff = CFPB complaint spike within 30 days.
